How I work / 04

Good work is a conversation.

The best builds come from sharing context early, making decisions visible, and leaving room for the work to teach us something.

A practical rhythm

Understand the problem, make a first version, learn from it, and keep the useful parts.

Less theatre.
More signal.

I’m comfortable working from a blank page, an existing interface, or a question that still needs to be shaped. I bring structure without pretending the answer is obvious on day one.

That means asking what matters, building a testable version, and communicating the trade-offs behind the work.

01

Listen first

Understand the people, constraints, and definition of a good outcome.

02

Make it visible

Use sketches, prototypes, and small working pieces to make ideas discussable.

03

Keep learning

Use feedback and evidence to improve the work instead of defending the first idea.
